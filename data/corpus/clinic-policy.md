# MicroHeal Clinic Policy

MicroHeal runs a wellness room and a visiting-clinician programme inside each partner residential society. Every society is attached to one partner hospital. This policy applies to all societies; each society's annex adds its own hours, covered services and escalation contacts, and the annex wins wherever the two differ.

## What the concierge does

The MicroHeal concierge, on WhatsApp and on the web, can:

- book a doctor consultation or a lab test with a MicroHeal clinician
- show you your upcoming and past bookings
- note a symptom you want the care team to know about, with a severity from 1 (mild) to 5 (severe)
- answer questions about the clinic using MicroHeal's published documents
- connect you to a nurse

### What the concierge does not do

The concierge is not a doctor. It does not diagnose, does not suggest medicines, and does not tell you how much of a medicine to take. For any of those, book a consultation. In an emergency it will not try to help you itself; it raises an emergency escalation to the care team and tells you to call 112 or your hospital's emergency desk.

## Booking appointments

### Doctor consultations

Consultations take place in the society wellness room or, where the annex says so, at home. A consultation is booked against a named clinician and a start time on that clinician's slot grid. Slots are 20, 30 or 45 minutes depending on the department.

A booking needs at least 2 hours' notice. Same-day bookings within 2 hours must be made by calling the wellness room.

### Lab tests

Lab tests are collected at home by a MicroHeal phlebotomist in a 15-minute collection slot, usually early morning. Some tests need fasting; see the Test Preparation and Fasting document. Reports are shared on WhatsApp and in the app.

### One booking per slot

A clinician can hold only one active booking in a given slot. If a slot is taken, the concierge offers the nearest free slots with the same clinician.

## Cancelling and rescheduling

You can cancel or reschedule free of charge up to 2 hours before the slot. A booking cancelled with less than 2 hours' notice, or a missed booking, counts as a no-show. Three no-shows in 90 days pause online booking for 30 days; bookings can still be made by calling the wellness room.

## Languages

The concierge and the care team work in English and Hindi. You can write in Hinglish. Replies come in the language you wrote in.

## Privacy and consent

### What we store

We store your name, flat number, phone number, date of birth, your bookings, the symptoms you log, and your conversations with the concierge. Lab reports are stored by the partner hospital, not by MicroHeal.

### Who can see it

Your records are visible to the MicroHeal care team for your society and to clinicians you have booked. Residents of other societies, and MicroHeal staff assigned to other societies, cannot see your records.

### Consent

By registering you consent to MicroHeal contacting you on WhatsApp about your bookings and reports. You can withdraw consent by writing "STOP" on WhatsApp; this stops reminders but does not delete your records. To ask for deletion, write to the wellness room. Records needed for an open emergency escalation are kept until the escalation is closed.

### Retention

Conversation transcripts are kept for 12 months. Bookings and symptom logs are kept for 3 years.
