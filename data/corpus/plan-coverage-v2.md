# Society Care Plan — What Is Covered

Every registered resident of a partner society is on the Society Care Plan. This document lists what the plan pays for from 1 April 2026. It replaces the previous version in full. Your society's annex may add services on top of these.

## What changed on 1 April 2026

- **Thyroid Profile is now covered** once per resident per plan year. It was previously not covered.
- **Vitamin D (25-OH) is no longer covered.** It is available at the member rate of ₹650.

Everything else is unchanged.

## Doctor consultations

### General physician

Consultations with a general physician are covered without limit.

### Specialists

Two specialist consultations per resident per quarter are covered. Further specialist consultations cost ₹400 each at the member rate. Specialist departments are Cardiology, Paediatrics, Orthopaedics, Dermatology, ENT and Gynaecology.

## Lab tests

### Covered every quarter

- Complete Blood Count (CBC)
- Fasting Blood Sugar (FBS)
- HbA1c

### Covered once a year

- Lipid Profile
- Liver Function Test (LFT)
- Kidney Function Test (KFT)
- Thyroid Profile (T3, T4, TSH) — covered once per resident per plan year

### Not covered

- Vitamin D (25-OH) — not covered; member rate ₹650
- Urine Routine — not covered; member rate ₹150

## Plan year

The plan year runs from 1 April to 31 March. Unused covered tests do not carry over.

## Members aged 60 and over

Residents aged 60 and over get one additional covered general check-up visit per quarter, with CBC and FBS included.
