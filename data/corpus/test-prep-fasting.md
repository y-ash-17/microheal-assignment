# Test Preparation and Fasting

This page tells you how to prepare for a home sample collection. It is about the collection procedure only. It does not tell you whether you need a test or what any result means; your doctor decides both.

## Which tests need fasting

### Fasting needed

| Test | Fasting | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Lipid Profile | 10–12 hours | Book an early-morning slot. |
| Fasting Blood Sugar (FBS) | 8–10 hours | Book an early-morning slot. |

### No fasting needed

CBC, HbA1c, Thyroid Profile, Vitamin D, Liver Function Test, Kidney Function Test and Urine Routine do not need fasting. Thyroid Profile is best collected in the morning.

### More than one test in the same collection

If any test in the collection needs fasting, fast for the longest period among them. One collection slot covers all tests booked for that morning.

## During the fast

- Plain water is allowed and encouraged.
- No tea, coffee, milk, juice, chewing gum or smoking.
- Avoid heavy exercise on the morning of the test.

## Medicines

Do not stop, skip or change any medicine because of a test unless your doctor has told you to. If you take medicine for diabetes, blood pressure or thyroid and are unsure whether to take your morning dose before the collection, ask the doctor who prescribed it. The concierge and the phlebotomist cannot advise on this.

## Urine samples

Use the sterile container the phlebotomist leaves the evening before. Collect the first urine of the morning, midstream.

## On the day

Keep your booking confirmation and a photo ID ready. The phlebotomist will confirm your name and date of birth before collecting. If you have eaten by mistake, tell the phlebotomist; they will reschedule the fasting tests at no charge and collect the others.

## Reports

Turnaround times are listed in the Department and Test Directory. Reports arrive on WhatsApp. Questions about what a report means should go to a doctor consultation.
