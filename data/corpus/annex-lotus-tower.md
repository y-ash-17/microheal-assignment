# Society Annex — Lotus Tower

Lotus Tower, Sector 62, Noida. Partner hospital: **Arogya Multispeciality Hospital**, Sector 61, Noida. This annex applies only to residents of Lotus Tower and takes precedence over the Clinic Policy where the two differ.

## Covered services at Lotus Tower

These are in addition to the Society Care Plan.

- **Home sample collection costs ₹150 per visit.** It is free for residents aged 60 and over.
- **Dietitian teleconsultation:** 4 per resident per plan year are covered. Book them by calling the wellness room; they cannot be booked through the concierge.

Physiotherapy is not offered at Lotus Tower.
