# Society Annex — Royal Palms

Royal Palms, Sector 54, Gurugram. Partner hospital: **Sanjeevani Hospital**, Sector 44, Gurugram. This annex applies only to residents of Royal Palms and takes precedence over the Clinic Policy where the two differ.

| Sunday | 09:00 – 13:00 |
| Public holidays | 09:00 – 13:00 |

Wellness room phone: +91 124 4000 2301.

## Covered services at Royal Palms

These are in addition to the Society Care Plan.

- **Home sample collection is free** for every resident, for every `LAB` booking.
- **Home physiotherapy:** 6 sessions per resident per plan year are covered. Further sessions cost ₹700 each.

Dietitian consultations are not offered at Royal Palms.
