# Society Annex — Shivalik Greens

Shivalik Greens, Sector 49, Gurugram. Partner hospital: **Sanjeevani Hospital**, Sector 44, Gurugram. This annex applies only to residents of Shivalik Greens and takes precedence over the Clinic Policy where the two differ.

## Covered services at Shivalik Greens

These are in addition to the Society Care Plan.

- **Home sample collection is free** for every resident, for every `LAB` booking.
- **Home physiotherapy:** 6 sessions per resident per plan year are covered. Further sessions cost ₹700 each.
- **Eye screening camp:** once a year, in October, in the clubhouse.

Dietitian consultations are not offered at Shivalik Greens.
