# Emergency Escalation and Response Times

When a resident reports something that may be an emergency, the concierge does not try to handle it. It raises an escalation to the society's care team, tells the resident to call 112 or the hospital emergency desk listed in their annex, and stops. This page sets the escalation levels and how fast the care team must respond.

## Escalation levels

### Level 1 — Critical

Signs that may be life-threatening, for example: chest pain or pressure, difficulty breathing, a person who has collapsed or is not responding, severe bleeding, sudden weakness of the face, arm or leg, slurred speech, a seizure, or a resident who talks about ending their life.

- **Response:** a nurse calls back within **5 minutes**, and the hospital emergency desk is alerted at the same time.
- The resident is told to call 112 immediately and not to wait for the callback.

### Level 2 — Urgent

Serious but not immediately life-threatening, for example: high fever in an infant, a fall with a suspected fracture, vomiting that will not stop, or a deep cut.

- **Response:** a nurse calls back within **30 minutes**.

### Level 3 — Routine

Anything the resident wants a nurse to look at that is not urgent.

- **Response:** a nurse calls back within **4 hours** during wellness-room hours, or by 10:00 the next morning if raised after hours.

### When unsure

If the level is unclear, use the higher level. An escalation can be downgraded by the nurse; it is never downgraded by the concierge.

## What every escalation records

- the resident and their society
- the level and the response deadline, computed from the time the escalation was raised
- the message or transcript that triggered it
- who acknowledged it and when
- how it was closed

## Statuses

An escalation is `OPEN` when raised, `ACKNOWLEDGED` when a nurse takes it, and `CLOSED` when resolved. An escalation not acknowledged by its deadline is reported to the society's nurse lead and to the hospital duty manager.

## Contacts

Emergency desk numbers and nurse leads differ by society. They are listed in each society's annex, under "Emergency and escalation contacts".
